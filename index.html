Exclusive Report: Fortnite Whistleblower Reveals True Reason Behind Mass Layoffs

By Frank Williams | Tuesday, May 19, 2026 | 8:42 AM

RALEIGH, NC (WVTD) -- A recently terminated employee from Epic Games is speaking out after a recent round of layoffs, claiming that an unexpected source contributed heavily to the company’s internal chaos: a single Fortnite gamer whose IP address showed they were based in Alabama and who filed over 10,000 cheating reports over the course of several years.

The former employee, who requested anonymity and referred to himself only as “Anonymous304” claims the flood of reports created “an absolute nightmare” for moderation staff and overloaded internal systems designed to process cheating investigations.

“At first everyone thought it was a coordinated bot attack,” the whistleblower said. “Then we realized it was mostly one guy. One very committed guy.”

According to Anonymous304, the Alabama-based gamer reported nearly every opponent who eliminated him in-game, accusing players of having “Obvious Aimbot installed,” “Tracking him through terrain,” or using what he referred to as “Walmart Hacks.” Investigators found that only around 2% of the reports were ultimately considered credible. At one point, the user even reported Ninja, the famous streamer, after an embarrassing loss.

Sources familiar with the situation claim the backlog became so severe that server allocations had to be reduced during several peak periods, leading to degraded matchmaking performance and increased operational costs. Employees reportedly spent thousands of hours reviewing and processing the ongoing stream of reports.

“The system wasn’t built for somebody treating the report button like a machine gun,” he said. “There were multiple times when the queue backed up so fast it looked like everyone in the lobby suddenly discovered the report button was a mythic item.”

Industry analysts have questioned the plausibility of the claims, but Anonymous304 insists upper management quietly acknowledged the issue internally months before the layoffs began.

“People think the layoffs were because of the economy or declining player counts,” he stated. “No. It was because moderation teams were drowning in reports from one guy in Alabama who genuinely believed every 12-year-old with decent aim was hacking.”

The individual has not been publicly identified, though Anonymous304 claims he is now the subject of an ongoing internal investigation and possible civil action related to operational damages.

Representatives for Epic Games declined to comment directly on the allegations but issued a brief statement:

“We continuously evaluate our moderation systems to ensure reports are handled efficiently and fairly,” the representative said, while also issuing a reminder to players that the report feature is “not intended to function as a primary gameplay mechanic.”

Meanwhile, players online have already turned the story into a meme, with hashtags like #ReportRoyale and #ReportButtonWarrior trending across gaming forums.

One viral post joked:

“Bro got so mad after losing at Fortnite he accidentally downsized a corporation.”

As of publication, no official lawsuit has been filed, though insiders claim legal teams are “monitoring the situation closely.”
